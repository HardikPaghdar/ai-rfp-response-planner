REQUEST FOR PROPOSAL: CUSTOMER OPERATIONS PLATFORM

1. The supplier must provide a secure web application for customer operations teams.
2. The solution shall support role-based access control for administrators, managers, and agents.
3. The platform must integrate with existing REST APIs and support secure data exchange.
4. The service must maintain 99.9% monthly availability and provide incident notifications.
5. The supplier should provide functional testing, regression testing, and release validation documentation.
6. The implementation team must provide a delivery plan with milestones, dependencies, and named owners.
7. Customer data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
8. The supplier shall provide ongoing technical support, issue triage, and maintenance.
9. The supplier must demonstrate compliance with the customer's data residency requirements.
10. The solution should provide audit logs for administrative actions and changes to customer records.

Evaluation notes:
- Provide a written response for each numbered requirement.
- Reference supporting evidence where available.
- Identify any requirement that cannot currently be met.
- Do not make unsupported claims about certifications, service levels, or customer references.
